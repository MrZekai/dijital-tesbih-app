Tasbeeh Counter: Dhikr & Zikr

Digital tasbih: tap to count your dhikr and feel a vibration at your target.

Tasbeeh Counter is a digital tasbih for counting dhikr on a calm screen. Tap the large counter circle to count, including with multiple fingers.

COUNT YOUR DHIKR
• Choose a target of 33, 99, 100, 500 or 1000, or enter your own
• See how many counts remain and track completed rounds
• Undo a count when you need to
• Choose whether to use vibration and bead sounds
• Create your own dhikrs, add Arabic text when available, and find favorites and recently used dhikrs

AFTER-PRAYER DHIKR
Follow 33 SubhanAllah, 33 Alhamdulillah and 33 Allahu Akbar in order. If you leave partway through, you can resume within 12 hours of your last count.

THE 99 NAMES
Browse the 99 Names of Allah with Arabic writing, transliteration and a counter for each name. Search and save favorites. Meanings are available in Turkish and English; other interface languages show English meanings.

YOUR ROUTINE
See daily, weekly, monthly and all-time counts, a daily goal and streaks. Set an optional daily reminder. Choose a dark, light or system theme, large text, simple mode and keep-screen-awake setting. The interface supports 27 languages.

PRIVACY AND ADS
No account is needed. Your counts, dhikrs and history are stored on your device, and counting works without an internet connection. The app contains Google AdMob ads, which may process device and app usage data. See the Google Play Data safety section and privacy policy for details. You can export a backup file from Settings.
